# The fittlist ethos

fittlist exists to put people in rooms together. Success is a class attended,
a coach found, a friend made at the gym. It is never a minute spent in the app.

## The laws

In order. When two collide, the earlier one wins.

1. **Obey the law.**
2. **Take care of the people who train.** Members first, in every decision.
3. **Take care of the coaches.** Their schedule is their livelihood. Treat it
   that way.
4. **Take care of whoever builds this.** Paid fairly, given what they need,
   and they use the app too.

## The lines

Things fittlist does not do, at any size, for any reason.

- **Never addicting.** The app ends. Your week empties itself. No streaks, no
  infinite feeds, no reason to open it that isn't a real class with real
  people.
- **Nobody is the product.** No ads. Personal data is never sold, shared, or
  brokered. What people tell us is used to run the thing they asked for, and
  for nothing else.
- **Private by default.** A follow is private. Visibility is unlocked by
  consent, never by growth math.
- **No enshittification.** The product never gets worse on purpose. No feature
  is degraded, gated, or cluttered to squeeze value out of people who already
  trusted us.
- **Charge in the open.** When fittlist costs money, the price comes with the
  reason: what it pays for, and why it is what it is. Paying for the lights,
  never for the growth chart.
- **Few features, done well.** Build what helps someone find their fit. Skip
  the rest, even when it's easy.
- **Human, not artificial.** Fittlist is people teaching people, in rooms. AI
  helps build the product; it never performs in it. No sparkle glyphs, no
  generated coaches, no machine pretending to be a person. What you see on
  fittlist was done by someone.

## How to use this

Before an idea ships, it answers one question: which law does it serve? If it
serves none, it waits. If it breaks one, it dies, no matter whose idea it was
or how good the numbers look.

This file changes slowly and reluctantly. Adding a line is easy; living with
it is the commitment.
